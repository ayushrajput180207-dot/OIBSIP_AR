Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The Missile Man of India and the People's President

Introduction

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was a great scientist and the 11th President of India. He worked for many years on space and missile projects, and because of this he is called the "Missile Man of India". He was also very loved by students and common people, so everybody called him the "People's President". He was a simple, humble and hard-working person who always asked young people to dream big.

Birth and Education

  • Born: 15 October 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India.
  • Family: He grew up in a middle-class family. His father owned boats and was an imam of a local mosque.
  • School: Schwartz Higher Secondary School, Ramanathapuram.
  • College: St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, where he studied Physics (B.Sc.).
  • Engineering: Madras Institute of Technology (MIT), Chennai, where he studied Aerospace Engineering.
  • Passed away: 27 July 2015, in Shillong, while giving a lecture to students.

Important Achievements

  • Worked as a scientist at DRDO and ISRO for many years.
  • Was the project director of SLV-III, the first Indian-made satellite launch vehicle, which launched the Rohini satellite in 1980.
  • Played a major role in India's missile programme (Agni and Prithvi), so he became known as the "Missile Man of India".
  • Worked as Chief Scientific Adviser to the Prime Minister and Secretary of DRDO.
  • Played an important role in the Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998.
  • Became the 11th President of India (2002 to 2007).
  • Received Padma Bhushan (1981), Padma Vibhushan (1990) and Bharat Ratna (1997).
  • Wrote many inspiring books like Wings of Fire, India 2020 and Ignited Minds.
